# Beach Day: exact video prompts (Kling)

The keyframe image prompts are in the full script.

## Shot 1: Hot sand (`s1_hot_sand`)

> Bartholomew steps off the boardwalk onto the sand with a happy grin. Within the first second his eyes pop wide and his cheeks puff out as the sand is scorching hot. He hops quickly from one foot to the other, knees high, the rolled towel bouncing under his arm, then sprints away across the sand in fast little tiptoe steps toward the sea, glancing back at the camera with a pained, wide-eyed "ouch ouch" grimace. Camera: static medium-wide shot, eye level, the boardwalk edge and the sand in frame. Character performance: bouncy, curious, energetic little movements; wiggles, hops, quick head tilts; expressive face with big delighted smiles, wide-eyed surprise, happy squeezed-shut eyes, mischievous grins. Continuity: keep everything from the first frame; the same outfit and props stay on the whole time, same framing and size. the red-and-white striped swim trunks stay on, the straw sun hat with the pink ribbon stays on his head, the white sunblock stripe stays on his nose, and the striped towel stays rolled under his arm, all keeping their colors. The bear keeps the same curly caramel fur and brown suede nose throughout; his eyes always stay tiny glossy black beads, with no white eye whites and no cartoon eyes. Sound: seagulls, gentle waves, quick soft sandy footsteps, a little surprised "hoo-hoo". No voice, no speech, no giggling or laughing sounds (at most one brief soft chuckle). Style: Cinematic feature-film 3D bear with rich photoreal fur, in a stylized pastel storybook cartoon world with rounded arches, sparkles and glossy bubbles.

## Shot 2: Sandcastle (`s2_sandcastle`)

> Bartholomew gives the top of the tallest tower one last proud pat and turns to the camera with a big pleased grin, paws spread like "ta-da". Around the two-second mark the foamy wave rushes up the sand behind him, swirls around the castle and around him up to his tummy, and the whole sandcastle slumps and melts down into a flat smooth lump of wet sand. The water slides back out to sea. For the last two seconds he sits there soaked from the tummy down, water dripping from his hat brim, holding the little red spade, stares at the flat lump where the castle was, then looks at the camera with a flat, unimpressed pout. Camera: static medium shot, eye level, the bear, the whole sandcastle and the incoming wave in frame. Character performance: bouncy, curious, energetic little movements; wiggles, hops, quick head tilts; expressive face with big delighted smiles, wide-eyed surprise, happy squeezed-shut eyes, mischievous grins. Continuity: keep everything from the first frame; the same outfit and props stay on the whole time, same framing and size. the red-and-white striped swim trunks, the straw sun hat with the pink ribbon and the white sunblock stripe on his nose all stay on; the bucket stays red; there is only one sandcastle and it only melts down, it never moves or floats away. The bear keeps the same curly caramel fur and brown suede nose throughout; his eyes always stay tiny glossy black beads, with no white eye whites and no cartoon eyes. Sound: waves, a big rushing whoosh of a wave, fizzing foam, a soft sandy slump, water dripping, seagulls. No voice, no speech, no giggling or laughing sounds (at most one brief soft chuckle). Style: Cinematic feature-film 3D bear with rich photoreal fur, in a stylized pastel storybook cartoon world with rounded arches, sparkles and glossy bubbles.

## Shot 3: Crab pinch (`s3_crab_pinch`)

> Bartholomew lies sunbathing with a blissful smile. Within the first two seconds the little red crab scuttles sideways onto the towel and clamps one claw onto his big toe. His eyes snap open wide and he springs straight up onto his feet, hopping on one leg while holding the other foot up with the crab dangling from his toe. He shakes his foot hard and the crab lets go, drops onto the sand and scuttles away sideways. For the last second he clutches his toe, then glares after the crab with a grumpy, puffed-cheek pout. Camera: static medium-wide shot, eye level, the whole towel, the umbrella and the crab in frame. Character performance: bouncy, curious, energetic little movements; wiggles, hops, quick head tilts; expressive face with big delighted smiles, wide-eyed surprise, happy squeezed-shut eyes, mischievous grins. Continuity: keep everything from the first frame; the same outfit and props stay on the whole time, same framing and size. the red-and-white striped swim trunks, the straw sun hat with the pink ribbon and the white sunblock stripe on his nose all stay on; there is exactly one small red crab, it stays crab sized; the towel stays blue-and-white striped. The bear keeps the same curly caramel fur and brown suede nose throughout; his eyes always stay tiny glossy black beads, with no white eye whites and no cartoon eyes. Sound: gentle waves, tiny clicking crab claws, a squeaky "eep!", soft thumping hops on sand, seagulls. No voice, no speech, no giggling or laughing sounds (at most one brief soft chuckle). Style: Cinematic feature-film 3D bear with rich photoreal fur, in a stylized pastel storybook cartoon world with rounded arches, sparkles and glossy bubbles.

## Shot 4: Seagull thief (`s4_seagull_thief`)

> Bartholomew brings the honey sandwich toward his mouth with his eyes closed. Within the first second the seagull flaps up and clamps its beak onto the far end of the sandwich. His eyes snap open and he grips his end tight: a quick tug-of-war, the bear leaning back and the seagull flapping hard. Around the three-second mark the seagull gives one big yank, the bear is pulled forward off the towel and lands face-first in the sand, and the seagull flies off up into the sky with the whole sandwich. For the last second he lifts his head, his face covered in sand, paws empty, and gives the camera a flat, betrayed stare. Camera: static medium-wide shot, eye level, the bear, the towel and the seagull all in frame with room above for the seagull to fly away. Character performance: bouncy, curious, energetic little movements; wiggles, hops, quick head tilts; expressive face with big delighted smiles, wide-eyed surprise, happy squeezed-shut eyes, mischievous grins. Continuity: keep everything from the first frame; the same outfit and props stay on the whole time, same framing and size. the red-and-white striped swim trunks, the straw sun hat with the pink ribbon and the white sunblock stripe on his nose all stay on; there is exactly one seagull, white with a yellow beak, ordinary seagull sized; there is exactly one sandwich. The bear keeps the same curly caramel fur and brown suede nose throughout; his eyes always stay tiny glossy black beads, with no white eye whites and no cartoon eyes. Sound: seagull squawks, flapping wings, a stretchy tugging creak of bread, a soft sandy flump, waves. No voice, no speech, no giggling or laughing sounds (at most one brief soft chuckle). Style: Cinematic feature-film 3D bear with rich photoreal fur, in a stylized pastel storybook cartoon world with rounded arches, sparkles and glossy bubbles.

## Shot 5: Surf's up (`s5_surfs_up`)

> Bartholomew shoots up the face of the wave toward the curling lip, crouching lower to gather speed. Around the one-and-a-half-second mark he launches off the top of the wave into the air, the surfboard stuck to his feet, and spins one full 360-degree turn high above the wave against the glowing sunset sky, a glittering fan of spray and sparkles trailing behind the board. He lands back on the wave face with a big splash, bends his knees, steadies himself, and for the last two seconds glides down the wave in a cool relaxed stance, one paw raised in a "hang loose" wave at the camera with a huge proud grin. Camera: static wide shot, eye level, the whole wave, the bear and the sky above the wave all in frame with room for the jump. Character performance: bouncy, curious, energetic little movements; wiggles, hops, quick head tilts; expressive face with big delighted smiles, wide-eyed surprise, happy squeezed-shut eyes, mischievous grins. Continuity: keep everything from the first frame; the same outfit and props stay on the whole time, same framing and size. the red-and-white striped swim trunks, the straw sun hat with the pink ribbon and the white sunblock stripe on his nose all stay on, even during the spin; there is exactly one surfboard, it stays pastel mint green and stays under his feet the whole time. The bear keeps the same curly caramel fur and brown suede nose throughout; his eyes always stay tiny glossy black beads, with no white eye whites and no cartoon eyes. Sound: a rushing roar of the wave, a whoosh as he launches, a sparkly swish during the spin, a big splash landing, waves, distant seagulls. No voice, no speech, no giggling or laughing sounds (at most one brief soft chuckle). Style: Cinematic feature-film 3D bear with rich photoreal fur, in a stylized pastel storybook cartoon world with rounded arches, sparkles and glossy bubbles.
