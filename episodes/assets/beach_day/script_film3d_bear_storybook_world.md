# Bartholomew's Beach Day — script for approval

**Style:** Cinematic film bear in a storybook world · **Personality:** Playful · **Length:** ~23s, 9:16 · **Shots:** 5

**Estimated cost (first pass):** ~$4.76 (keyframes $0.90, video $3.61, audio $0.25)

## Story

1. **Hot sand** (3s): Bartholomew steps off the boardwalk onto the sand with a happy grin. Within the first second his eyes pop wide and his cheeks puff out as the sand is scorching hot. He hops quickly from one foot to the other, knees high, the rolled towel bouncing under his arm, then sprints away across the sand in fast little tiptoe steps toward the sea, glancing back at the camera with a pained, wide-eyed "ouch ouch" grimace.
2. **Sandcastle** (5s): Bartholomew gives the top of the tallest tower one last proud pat and turns to the camera with a big pleased grin, paws spread like "ta-da". Around the two-second mark the foamy wave rushes up the sand behind him, swirls around the castle and around him up to his tummy, and the whole sandcastle slumps and melts down into a flat smooth lump of wet sand. The water slides back out to sea. For the last two seconds he sits there soaked from the tummy down, water dripping from his hat brim, holding the little red spade, stares at the flat lump where the castle was, then looks at the camera with a flat, unimpressed pout.
3. **Crab pinch** (5s): Bartholomew lies sunbathing with a blissful smile. Within the first two seconds the little red crab scuttles sideways onto the towel and clamps one claw onto his big toe. His eyes snap open wide and he springs straight up onto his feet, hopping on one leg while holding the other foot up with the crab dangling from his toe. He shakes his foot hard and the crab lets go, drops onto the sand and scuttles away sideways. For the last second he clutches his toe, then glares after the crab with a grumpy, puffed-cheek pout.
4. **Seagull thief** (5s): Bartholomew brings the honey sandwich toward his mouth with his eyes closed. Within the first second the seagull flaps up and clamps its beak onto the far end of the sandwich. His eyes snap open and he grips his end tight: a quick tug-of-war, the bear leaning back and the seagull flapping hard. Around the three-second mark the seagull gives one big yank, the bear is pulled forward off the towel and lands face-first in the sand, and the seagull flies off up into the sky with the whole sandwich. For the last second he lifts his head, his face covered in sand, paws empty, and gives the camera a flat, betrayed stare.
5. **Surf's up** (5s): Bartholomew shoots up the face of the wave toward the curling lip, crouching lower to gather speed. Around the one-and-a-half-second mark he launches off the top of the wave into the air, the surfboard stuck to his feet, and spins one full 360-degree turn high above the wave against the glowing sunset sky, a glittering fan of spray and sparkles trailing behind the board. He lands back on the wave face with a big splash, bends his knees, steadies himself, and for the last two seconds glides down the wave in a cool relaxed stance, one paw raised in a "hang loose" wave at the camera with a huge proud grin.

**Music:** sunny breezy beach music with ukulele, steel drums, bouncy marimba and light hand percussion, building to a bright triumphant surf finale, instrumental, warm, loopable, no vocals, high quality

The character and style text repeats in every shot on purpose; it keeps the bear consistent.
