# Bartholomew's Spa Day — script for approval

**Style:** Cinematic film bear in a storybook world · **Personality:** Playful · **Length:** ~25s, 9:16 · **Shots:** 5

**Estimated cost (first pass):** ~$5.07 (keyframes $0.90, video $3.92, audio $0.25)

## Story

1. **Arrival** (5s): Bartholomew bounces up on tiptoes, taps the little bell with a paw, ding, then breaks into a huge delighted grin and wiggles with excitement
2. **Face mask** (5s): one cucumber slice slowly slides off; Bartholomew opens one eye, looks surprised, then grins mischievously, pops the cucumber in their mouth, and leans back satisfied
3. **Bubble bath** (5s): Bartholomew hops in with a cannonball splash, foam explodes upward, then pops back up wearing a big bubble crown on their head, giggling with eyes squeezed shut and paddling paws
4. **Hot stones** (5s): Bartholomew kicks their little feet happily in the air, wiggles, and melts into the table with a blissful squeezed-eye smile as warm steam drifts up
5. **Afterglow** (5s): Bartholomew takes a sip of tea, gives a big satisfied grin to the camera, then flops backward into the soft cushion with arms out, paws in the air

**Music:** light, bouncy, playful acoustic spa music with soft marimba and pizzicato, instrumental, warm, gentle, loopable, no vocals, high quality

## Exact prompts

The character and style text repeats in every shot on purpose; it keeps the bear consistent.
