# Bartholomew's Hiking Day — script for approval

**Style:** Cinematic film bear in a storybook world · **Personality:** Playful · **Length:** ~25s, 9:16 · **Shots:** 5

**Estimated cost (first pass):** ~$5.07 (keyframes $0.90, video $3.92, audio $0.25)

## Story

1. **Trailhead** (3s): Bartholomew takes one big excited breath, puffing his chest out even more, and gives a firm little nod at the camera. Within the first second he turns to face along the trail and starts marching away from the post with big exaggerated proud steps, knees lifting high and both arms swinging back and forth, the red backpack bouncing on his back with each step. After three quick steps he glances back over his shoulder at the camera with a big cheeky grin and keeps marching up the path.
2. **Stepping stones** (5s): Bartholomew hops onto the next stepping stone and wobbles, windmilling his arms, then steadies himself and gives a proud little smile. He hops to the last stone, but it tips under his foot, and within the first three seconds he slides off and plops bottom-first into the shallow stream with a big splash, sitting in water up to his tummy. Water drips from the brim of his hat; he blinks, looks down at the water, then looks up at the camera with a sheepish, soggy grin.
3. **Pinecone bonk** (7s): Within the first two seconds, one brown pinecone drops from the branches and bonks Bartholomew right on top of his bucket hat, squashing the hat down over his eyes; he wobbles, pushes the hat back up with one paw and blinks in surprise. A second pinecone falls and he lunges sideways and catches it in both paws, then holds it up toward the camera with a smug, triumphant grin. Behind his back the whole branch shakes and, around the four-second mark, a big shower of dozens of pinecones pours down on him all at once, burying him in a tall heap of brown pinecones right up to his chin. For the last two seconds only his head pokes out of the pile, one pinecone sitting on top of his bucket hat, and he gives the camera a flat, unimpressed look, then breaks into a sheepish grin.
4. **Steep hill** (5s): Bartholomew huffs and puffs and crawls a little higher up the steep grass, then his feet slip out from under him. He slides backward down the slope on his tummy, then curls into a round furry ball and rolls all the way to the bottom of the hill, his hat and backpack staying on as he tumbles. He lands sitting up in a soft patch of wildflowers at the bottom, a daisy stuck on his hat, blinks dizzily, then shakes his head, gives a little determined huff and stares back up the hill with a stubborn frown.
5. **Summit** (5s): Bartholomew raises the little pink flag high and plants it firmly into the grass at the top of the hill with both paws. He steps back, plants his fists on his hips and puffs out his chest in a proud hero pose while a warm breeze makes the flag flutter. Then he flops down to sit beside the flag on the tiny peak, legs stretched out in front of him, pulls a small round glass jar of golden honey with no label out of his backpack and pops off the lid. He dips one paw deep into the jar, scoops out a big gooey dollop of honey that drips in long golden strings, licks it off his paw with eyes closed in bliss, then hugs the honey jar and gazes out at the view with a sticky, contented smile.

**Music:** cheerful adventurous hiking music with bouncy acoustic guitar, whistling-style flute melody, light hand percussion and pizzicato strings, building to a warm triumphant ending, instrumental, warm, loopable, no vocals, high quality
