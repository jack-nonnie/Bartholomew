# Bartholomew's Gym Day — script for approval

**Style:** Cinematic film bear in a storybook world · **Personality:** Playful · **Length:** ~25s, 9:16 · **Shots:** 5

**Estimated cost (first pass):** ~$5.07 (keyframes $0.90, video $3.92, audio $0.25)

## Story

1. **Warm-up** (5s): Bartholomew bends down to touch his toes, wobbles back and forth, then tips over sideways onto the soft mat and pops right back up with a big proud grin
2. **Big lift** (5s): Bartholomew pushes the barbell proudly up over his head, then the red gummy bear slowly slides off the left end and lands on the mat with a wobbly jelly bounce; the bar tips toward the heavy green side and Bartholomew topples over sideways onto the mat, wide-eyed with surprise
3. **Treadmill** (5s): the treadmill starts and Bartholomew jogs, then it speeds up faster and faster until his little legs blur and can't keep up; his feet slip out from under him, he pitches forward and lands flat on his face on the moving belt, arms and legs splayed out like a starfish, and the belt carries him off the back end, sliding him face-down onto the soft blue mat; he lifts his head, blinks, and grins at the camera
4. **Jump rope** (5s): Bartholomew starts skipping the pink jump rope, faster and faster, until he misses a jump and the rope catches on his feet; as he spins around trying to untangle it, the rope coils around and around his round tummy and pins his arms to his sides, wrapping him snugly from shoulders to knees in neat pink loops like a wrapped parcel, the two handles dangling at his side; he hops in place all bundled up and gives the camera a sheepish grin
5. **Tuckered out** (5s): Bartholomew's eyes slowly droop, he gives a tiny yawn, then slowly tips over sideways onto the bench and falls asleep with a peaceful smile, still hugging the red gummy bear

**Music:** upbeat, bouncy, funny workout-montage music with plucky synth bass, hand claps and marimba, energetic but cute, gently winding down at the end, instrumental, warm, loopable, no vocals, high quality

## Exact prompts

The character and style text repeats in every shot on purpose; it keeps the bear consistent.
